[Washington,] August 8, 1945.

. . . . . . .

Mr. Novikov [주38] then told me informally and not as an official communication that in ten minutes from now, at three p.m. Washington time, 10 p.m., Moscow time, Mr. Molotov [주39] would make a declaration stating that Soviet Russia is in a state of war with Japan. [주40] I asked Mr. Novikov when the state of war would take effect and he said “tomorrow”. I said, “That will evidently give the Japanese two hours to get ready.”

Joseph C. Grew

----- 각주 -----
[주38] Nikolay Vassilyevich Novikov, Soviet Chargé.
[주39] Vyacheslav Mikhailovich Molotov, People’s Commissar for Foreign Affairs of the Soviet Union.
[주40] For statement handed by Commissar Molotov to the Japanese Ambassador in the Soviet Union, Naotake Sato, on August 8, see footnote 1, document 1382, Conference of Berlin (Potsdam), vol. ii, p. 1474.
