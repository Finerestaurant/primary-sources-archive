. Possible USSR base on Bear Island--According to Embassy London a 
high Foreign Office official believes that the Soviets will attempt to 


obtain from Norway base rights on Bear Island (midway between 
Spitzbergen and northern Norway). 


. Soviet exploitation of French and German Communists--According to — 
Caffrey, it now seems clear that Moscow is using the German and French 


Communist Parties as its ‘‘most valuable pawns’ In exploiting simul- 
taneously nationalist sentiments and the class struggle. Caffery com- 
ments that Stalin will lose only if both Parties fail eventually to domin- 
ate their respective countries. 


Spaniards discuss possible Stalin-Franco rapprochement--Caffery re- 
ports that both “Republican and pro-Franco Spaniards are discussing 


the possibility of a Soviet effort at rapprochement with Franco and be- 
lieve there is no reason why the USSR should not make such an effort 
by either political or commercial means. Caffery. adds that he has no 
evidence of a Soviet move in this direction. . 


Increased privileges for US at Lagens--General Kissner reports from 
Santa Maria that a new conciliatory attitude on the part of Portuguese. 
military authorities in the Azores has now assured the US a temporary 
contiruation at Lagens of essentially the same privileges which have 
been enjoyed at Santa Maria. The 30 May agreement had merely speci- 
fied ‘‘in transit through”’ rights for Lagens. 


Arab Ministers uncertain about future strategy--US Legation Cairo re- 


ports that the Arab Foreign Ministers face the future with misgivings 
and apparently have no program other than submission of the Palestine 
question to the UN in case, as they anticipate, the London Conference 
breaks down. The local Arab press is increasing its criticism of the 
Arab League as a ‘‘do nothing’’ organization.
