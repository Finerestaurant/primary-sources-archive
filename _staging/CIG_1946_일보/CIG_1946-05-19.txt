4, FRANCE: Reasons for defeat of Constitution--According to Caffery, the ; 
defeat of the draft Constitution is attributed to the unexpected defection 
of many Socialists from their Party line, and the notably few abstentions. 
The Communists overplayed their hand, and the referendum was in effect 
a plebiscite for or against Communism, rather than a constitutional issue. 
Caffery warns, however, that the rejection should not be interpreted as an 
important shift to the Right, since Commumists and Socialists together 
still control 47 percent of the electorate.
