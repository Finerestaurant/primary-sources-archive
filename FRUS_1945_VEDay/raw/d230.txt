London, May 10, 1945—8 p.m. 
 [Received May 10—5:30 p.m.]

4688. Comea 234. The UK Representative on the European Advisory Commission has circulated a memorandum regarding giving effect to paragraph 9 of the report of the EAC of Nov. 14, 1944, [주40] transmitting the agreement on control machinery in Germany. UK proposes that a public statement be made that in connection with the exercise by the four occupying powers of supreme authority with respect to Germany the four Governments intend to consult with the Governments of other United Nations. Suitable modification of wording is proposed, depending on whether the surrender instrument or the surrender declaration is issued by the four Governments. Full text by air. [주41]

Winant
