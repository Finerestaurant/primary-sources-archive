London, May 1, 1945—midnight.
[Received May 1—11:16 p.m.]

4421. Today the EAC approved for submission to the four Governments (1) a protocol including the French signatories to the instrument of unconditional surrender of Germany [주95] and (2) a protocol adding France to the other three Allies in the control machinery agreement for Germany. [주96] Originals by air to Department.

Repeated Paris as 232.

Winant
