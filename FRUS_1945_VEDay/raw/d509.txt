Bern, April 25, 1945.
[Received April 25—5 p.m.]

2438. Your 1507, April 19th. [주12] Foreign Office note April 24 states message telegraphed Swiss Legation Berlin. Additionally, in accordance desire of Department, Foreign Office transmitted aide-mémoire containing terms of German proposal and acceptance thereof by United States to German Legation Bern. This aide-mémoire dated April 21 delivered same day German Legation.

Harrison
