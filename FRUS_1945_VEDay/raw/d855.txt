Washington, April 6, 1945—8 a.m.

The unilateral procedure outlined in Deptelcirc March 31, 3 p.m. does not apply in countries to which you are accredited and reference was for your information only. However, you should keep the Department informed of any disposition made of such records by the countries to which you are accredited.

Stettinius
