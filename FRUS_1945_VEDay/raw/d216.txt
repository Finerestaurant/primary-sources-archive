Caffery has been instructed to clarify certain areas with General Eisenhower and then informally to communicate this new proposal to the French Government.

As soon as you hear from Caffery it is desired that you negotiate with your French colleague a protocol based on (1) the area to be [276면] described to you by Caffery and (2) the views expressed in memorandum of the Joint Chiefs of Staff dated April 27 [주23] and sent to you in Department’s air mail instruction 5389, April 30, [주24] except for statements contained in the last two sentences of paragraph 7b (3) and paragraph 8 which have now been superseded.
