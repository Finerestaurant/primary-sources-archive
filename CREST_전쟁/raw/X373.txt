1. The Chinese Communists plan to issue the following winter equipment to 
each soldier in Korea: A fur coat or padded gowns a cotton-padded uniform; 
one fur or cotton-padded hats two pairs of wool or cotton socks; one pair 
of leather or padded shoes; and one pair of cotton gloves. Each unit will 
receive 30 catties of coal per soldier. 


2. In April 1951 the Supply and Service Command of the Chinese Communist Army 
Headquarters began preparations to issue winter equipment, using the slogan 
"We must not let a single man get frostbitten," In August it issned a 
slogan guaranteeing the issue of winter equipment on time, As of 15 September 
it was urging the military supply department and the transport department 
to procure and ship winter equipment early. 


3. Since late August several consignments of winter equipment have been 
shipped to North Korea. One including fur hats and boots was from the 
Northeast, and another was from Peiping. A consignment from the Northwest 
which was in Central and “ast China was to be assembled in Peiping and 
Tientsin before shipment to Korea. The Supply and Service Command has 
estimated that a total of 31,000 tons of winter equipment was to be sent 
to Korea, and that all should be shipped before 20 October.
