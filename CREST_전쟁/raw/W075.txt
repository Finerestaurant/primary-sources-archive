NORTH KOREANS PROMOTS ‘FRIENDSHIP WITH USSR; 
INTENSIFY THOUGHT CONTROL EFFORTS 


ENCOURAGE RACIAL ASSIMILATION -- Sopuk Sinmun; 5 Jun 50 


The government of the People's Republic has been encouraging its girls to 
marry or live temporarily with the Soviet citizens in North Korea to promote 
closer friendship with the USSR through racial assimilation. The idea closely 
resembles the method employed by the Japanese before liberation in their effort 
to assimilate the Korean people through encouragement of interracial marriage. 


An extensive campaign has been launched by the government through various 
women's organizations to induce young women to mate with the Russians wider the 
slogan, "For the Promotion of Russo-Korean Friendship." Pressure has been ex- 
erted on schools, where social programs are arranged by the authorities to give 
girl students opportunities to meet the Russians.

too ignorant of or' too indifferent to the teaching of Communisn. These girls 
were rcewnded up by the authorities and given an opportunity to receive Commu-

yeog, P'yongan Namdo, and P'yonggang, Kangwon Do. When the indoctrination
