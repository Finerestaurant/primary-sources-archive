NORTH KOREAN STATE-OPERATED SHIPYARD FOR FISHING VESSELS 


According to Ordinance No 16, of the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, 
the state-operated shipyard for fishing vessels (hereinafter referred to as ship- 
yard) shall build state-owned vessels for the marine products industry. This 
shipyard is located on the premises of the state-operated Marine Industry Enter- 
prise, and is under the control of the Bureau of Marine Industry Administration, 
Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry. This shipyard shall perform the following 
functions: = , 


1.‘ Build dnd repair state-owned fishing vessels. 
2. “Manufacture shipbuilding materials, both of wood and metal. 


3. Develop shipbuilding technology. 


The director of the shipyard shall be appointed through the recommendation 
of the director ofthe Bureau of Marine Industry Administration. The administra- 
tionnof the shipyard is performed by its three departments: accounting, produc- 
tion, and‘planning. The planning department, among other things,- shall safeguard 
the secreta ef this shipyard.
