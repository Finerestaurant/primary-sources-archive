the most damage to the North Korean railroad system during 1951, but 
North Korean rail transport officers believe that the most effective 
disruption would be caused by the destruction of the water tanks supply-

be repaired within 10 days. Occasionally small groups of United States 
prisoners of war are in repair gangs. An average of one train per night, 
carrying grain northward for army use, travels this line; the train has 
been attacked on about 10 percent of its trips. The most effective
