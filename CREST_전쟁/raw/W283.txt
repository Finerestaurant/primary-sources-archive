RESEARCH INSTITUTES AND GRADUATE STUDY IN NORTH KOREA 


As provided for by Ordinance No 3, Ministry of Education, research insti- 
tutes shall be established according to the following regulations: 


A. General Provisions 


Research institutes shall be established in universities py order of the 
Ministry of Education, to train scientists, professors, and executives. 


Total enrollment and required subjects of study at each research institute 
shall be determined by the Ministry of Education. 


B. Entrance Requirenents 


A graduate student who is under 40 years of age and a citizen of the Demo- 
cratic People's Republic of Korea may be admitted to a research institute if he 
passes the entrance examinations covering the following subjects: fundamentals 
of Marxism-Leninism, political economy, two subjects in his major field of study 
and two foreign languages (Russian and either English, German, or French). 


Qualified students will be admitted upon recommendation of the university 
president or dean, and upon approval of the Ministry of Education. Students with 
distinctly superior academic records, or those who have had at least two years 
of actual training in their undergraduate major, will be given special considera-
