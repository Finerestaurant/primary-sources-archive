Highlights Date: sy: Pes 


The US I Corps and the ROK II Cerps continued to advance toward 
Pyongyang in the west coast area, capturing Hacju, Sinmak, Kuam, 
and Suan. Elements of the ROK I Corps entered the North Korean

Ground 


The US 24th Division attacking west and northwest toward Pyong- 
yang captured the port city of Haeju and threatened Sariwon. The US 
ist Cavalry Division overcame moderate enemy resistance to seize 
the town of Sinmak, and the 7th Cavalry Regiment continued north- 
west and captured the town of Kuam. The ROK ist Division overcame 
unusually heavy resistance from an estimated regiment and captured 
Suan town, with much enemy equipment and many prisoners. The 
ROK I Corps continued to advance into mountainous central North 
Korea against little enemy resistance. A regiment of the ROK Capital 
Bivision moved into the east coast industrial city of Hamhung; another 
regiment of the Capital Division advanced 13 miles westward along the 
road from Yonghung. In South Korea, ROK police report that the 
North Koreans still control the towns of Samechek and Ulchin on the 
east coast, and four small towns in the southwestern area. 


Navy 


Major elements of the UN fleet continued to patrol along the east 
coast in the Wonsan area. Carrier aircraft attacked communication 
! targets in northeast Korea. Other UN naval forces attacked mine 
depots and suspected minelayers on the Korean west coast.
