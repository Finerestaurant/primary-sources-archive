ANNOUNCES KANG KYON KILLED IN ACTION, 
WAS. NORTH KOREAN COMMANDER IN CHIEF 


Feiping, 13 September (Hsin-hua dispatch frox P'yongyang) -- The 
tentral Committee of the North Korean Labor Party, the Headquarters of 
the People's Army, the Presidium of the Supreme People's Assembly, the 
Cabinet of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, and the Democratic 
Front for Unification of the Fatherland jointly announced that Kang Kyon, 
vice-minister of the Ministry of National Defense and commander in chief 
of the Peovle's Army of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, was 
killed in action at the front on 8 September 1950. 


He led anti-Japanese guerrilla forces during his youth, was one of 
the organizers of the People's Army, a close friend of Premier Kim Il- 
song, and a member of the Supreme People's Assembly of the People's Repub- 
lic and of the Central Committee of the North Korean Labor Party.
