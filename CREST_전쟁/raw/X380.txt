CHINESE COMMUNISTS MAKE PREPARATIONS 
TO CONTINUE KOREAN CONFLICT 


WILL BOMB JAPAN IF UN STRIKES NORTHEAST -- Hong Kong, Kung-shang Jih-pao, 
31 Aug 51 


Taihoku, 30 August -- Chung-lien She reports that on the basis of a recent 
Sino-Soviet secret agreement, Soviet planes flying under the banner of the 
Chinese Communist Forces will bomb Japan if UN bombers strike the Northeast. 
Some 2,000 aircraft are said to be assembled in the Northeast and North Korea 
in readiness for action. 


According to Chung-lien She, 20,000 Japanese POW artillery men are assem- 
bled at Mukden. With them are a group of medical personnel selected from the 
Northeast and North China Chinese Communist Forces, who have received orders 
to be transferred to Korea. 


CHINESE TO GET LARGE QUANTITIES OF SOVIET EQUIPMENT -- Hong Kong, Hsing-tao 
Jih-pao, 29 Aug 51 


A Taihoku intelligence source reveals that according to a new Peiping-Moscow 
agreement, the Chinese Communists are to receive from the Soviet Union 2,000 air- 
craft, 1,000 heavy artillery pieces, and 600 tenks, and that the Chinese Commu- 
nist forces are to continue to be responsible for supplying men for the Korean 
conflict. The Chinese Communists, Chung-lien She reports, have 700,000 men in 
Korea of who 350,000 are on the front lines. 


TROOPS FROM USSR TRAIN FOR KOREA -- Hanoi, L'intente, 6 Sep 51 
According to a reliable source, & Large number of long trains have crossed 


the Sino-Soviet border since last spring. Each of these trains carried either 
2,000 men or tanks.

none has been in combat in Korea up to now.
