NIEH JUNG-CHEN FORCES MOVING TO NORTHEAST -- Hong Kong Kung-shang Jih-pao, 
3 Jul 50 


Taihoku, 2 July -- It is reported that the Conmunists are now transferring 
the forces of Nieh Jung-chen stationed in North China to the Northeast so that 
if and when China decides to aid the North Korean Army, this force will be on 
hand to move into North Korea. 


The report further stated that a large number of Communist forces are being 
transferred to the north to relieve Liu Po-ch'eng's troops which are being trans- 
ferred farther north with the Peiping-Tientsin troops. The coastal area opposite 
Taiwan is now being defended by the Fukien-Kiangsi-Chekiang forces of Ch'en I.

27 Jun 50

of Syngman Rhee's puppet government attacked the Republic of Korea by penetrat-

entire nation to suppress the enemy.
