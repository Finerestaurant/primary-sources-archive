1. Since 1 August Mongolian troops have been stationed along the Korean- 
Manchurian border, In late August these troops were incorporated into 
Chinese Communist divisions at the rate of 30 per company. 


2. On 26 and 27 August one regiment of Czechoslovakian "volunteers" were 
stationed in caves surrounding P'yongyang. These troops were fully 
armed, their weapons including artillery and heavy machine guns. The 
troops were transported by train from Sinuiju during the nights of 22, 
23, and 2 August and anticipated orders moving them to the western 
front. Five more divisions were said to be massing at Manp'ojin 
(126-17, 41-09} (BA 9259) and Sinuiju and were expected to move down 
to the western and central fronts. 


3. On 20 August 250 Soviet advisers arrived in P'yongyang for assignment 
to Chinese Comuunist and North Korean units at as low as company level. 
With the increase in Soviet advisers, military initiative has been 
eurtailed and the independence of the treops restricted,
