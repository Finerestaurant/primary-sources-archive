advisers are now holding important command positions in the North Korean Arny 
and that they are actively participating in the war. : 


The reporter stated that he obtained the following additional information

18 April 1950.

in the Chinese civil war.

somewhere in North Korea for one month and later infiltrated the eastern coast © 
of South Korea to carry out guerrilla activities.
