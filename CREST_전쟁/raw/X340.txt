Chinese plan for attack on Seoul 


1. On 15 August 1951, PENG Te-huai, commander-in-chief of the Chinese 
Communist volunteers, and several unidentified Soviet advisers went to 
Ich'on (126=53, 38-30) from Pyongyang on an inspection trip. PENG's 

plan was to establish an advance command post at Ichton and to concentrate 
the main army strength there for a drive against Seoul after the cease- 
fire negotiations break down, 


Joint air force command post at P'yongyang 


2. The Chinese Communists and North Korean Communists have reportedly 
set up an air force advance command post at P'yongyang. On 21 August 1951, 
LIU Ya-lou, commander~in-chief of the Chinese Communist Air Force, and 
Colonel Po-lung-k'o, a Soviet adviser, went from Mukden to Pyongyang 

on an inspection trip. All airfields in North Korea are expected to be 
serviceable by the end of September 1951.
