SUBJECT: Comments on SE=20;: "The Probable Consequences of Certain Possible 
US Courses of Action with Respect to Communist China and Korea'", 
draft dated 1h December 1951, 


1. This draft does not deal with the probable consequences of 
developments in Korea and the Far East on Western Kurope. I think 
that this is a serious omission which makes the report both incomplete 
and misleading. The possible US courses of action outlined in this 
draft would probably have important political, psychological and 
military repercussions on Western Europe which would affect US and 
NATO programs and the position of the US in its relations with the 
countries of Western Europe. On balance, I should guess that these 
US courses of action would damage US interests in Europe, possibly 
very seriously. The statement that "an increase of Soviet commit 
ments in the Far East would reduce Soviet capabilities elsewhere" 
(page 8, lines 6and 7), while technically correct, is misleading. 


2. I suggest one of the following: 


a. An examination of the effects of possible developments in 

Korea and China on the global interests of the USSR. This
