CHINESE EDUCATION IN NORTH. KOREA 
CABINET DECISION NO. 30, 19: 5) 


The Japanese colonization policy had literally denied Chinese children 
in Korea the right to have an adequate education. Prior to the liberation, 
there were only about 30 schools in Korea capable of accommodating, at the 
most, 1,000 Chinese children. This picture has now been changed. Today 
there are 6,000 Chinese children in 87 different schools in. North Korea. 
This figure does not include a number of high schools established recently 
for Chinese residents through the North korea People's Committee. 


Although educational opportunity for Chinese children has been sub- 
stantially increased, nevertheless, the material foundation and teacher- 
training programs still remain critical. In this connection the Northeast 
Administrative Committee of China and the Association of Chinese residents 
in {North/ Korea requested the Democratic People's Republic of Korea to 
take some measures to improve educational situation of the Chinese in North 
Korea: In compliance with this request the Cabinet of the Democratic Peo- 
ple's Republic of Korea has decided the following: 


1. Effective 1 April 1949, the administration of 811 Chinese schools 
in North Korea shall be detached from the Association of Chinese residents 
and entrusted in the hands of the Ministry of Education of the Democratic 
People's Republic of Korea. 


. 2. The responsibility of safeguarding Chinese school buildings and 
equipment in P'yongyang City and Sinviju until the end of July 1949 is in 
the hands of the Minister of Education, P'yongyang . People's Committee , 
P'yongan Pukto People's Committee, and Chogang-do People's Committee. 


3. To bring about more efficient administration of the Chinese educa- 
tional system in North Korea, 4 permanent staff of five will be appointed 
in the Bureau of Elementary Education in the Ministry of Education. ‘The 
sole function of this staff will be to supervise Chinese schools. In addi- 
tion, every province in North Korea shall appoint a full time educational
