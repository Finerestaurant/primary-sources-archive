Seoul Area. Heavy enemy resistance continues in the Seoul suburb 
of Yongdungpo on the south bank of the Han River, and Marines on the 
north bank are meeting stiff enemy resistance on the northwestern 
outskirts of Seoul, The otcupation of Suwon by US 7th Division 
troops on the main north-south highway and railroad 20 miles south of 
Seoul, combined with the Marine position across the railroad northwest 

_ of Seoul, leaves only one other route from south-central Korea open 


to enemy reinforcements attempting to enter the city. 


Southeastern Area, On the front near Masan, the US 25th Division 
pushed forward up to 6,000 yards toward Chinju against light enemy 
opposition, but stiff enemy resistarice prevented forward movement of

Division zone, accurate enemy mortar and artillery fire forced the |

elements of the 2nd Division expanded their bridgeheads west of the 
Naktong against moderate to heavy enemy resistance, In the Taegu area,
