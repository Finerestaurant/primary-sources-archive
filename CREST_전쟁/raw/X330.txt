Subject: Soviet and ‘International Volunteer’’ Forces in China, 
. Manchuria and Korea 


Since the beginning of the Korean war, there has been an 
increasing number of reports of Soviet troops in Communist 
China, Manchuria, and Korea. The accumulated evidence 
available to CIA does not confirm the existence of Soviet line 
combat units in these areas

Communist military forces alone.
