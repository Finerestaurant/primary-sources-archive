25X14 On about 7 January a North Korean artillery unit totalling about 7,600 men 
re-entered North Korea from China, The unit was trained and equipped by the 
Chinese Communist Army, The platoon commanders of the unit are Koreans who 
previously served in the Special Army Groups of the Chinese Communist 3 and 
4 Field Armies, Their equipment included 12 field pieces, 20 mountain guns, 
many heavy field pieces, mortars and chemical guns (sic), totalling 117 pleces. 
The unit officers had 80 days' training before going into Korea.
