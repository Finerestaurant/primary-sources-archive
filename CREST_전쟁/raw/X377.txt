NORTH KOREANS CHARGE USE OF CHEMICAL WARF ARE -- Sofia, Trud, 15 Aug 51 


Peiping, 14 August (Tass) -- According to a Hsin-hua News Agency correspond 
ent in P'yongyang, the North Korean Minister of Foreign Affairs, Pak Hun-yong, 
has protested to the UN on the use of chemical warfare pombs by the US Air Force. 
The protest jncluded the following: 


"On 1 August, at 1630 hours, US aircraft dropped two 300-kilogram chemical 

warfare bombs on Yonan. Three soldiers and hO civilians of thet town were poi- 

- soned; nine of these were severely affected. The symptoms of the afflicted per- 
sons included fever, tears, coughing, and vomiting. On 7 August, at 0630 hours, 
US aircraft dropped chemical warfare bombs on the Tunam Mountain area and on the 
Sudo Plateau, both of which have been captured by the North Korean Army. of the 
22 poisoned soldiers, eight died immediately. The American air force used chemi- 
cal warfare bombs against the North Korean Army, the Chinese volunteers, and 


other units at the front." 


The protest emphasized that these new crimes, which were conceived and 
perpetrated by the American invaders during the peace talks, demonstrate clearly 
their attitude towards making peace in Korea. The protest states that it is 
generally known that the American invaders use the name of the UN to conceal 
their crimes in Korea and that 18 nations, which upon the request. of the Ameri- 
cads have sent their troops to Korea, are accomplices in these crimes.
