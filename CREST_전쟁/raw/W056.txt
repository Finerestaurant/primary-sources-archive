NORTH KOREA RESETTLES FIRE-FIELD WORKERS IN FACTORIES 


TO PROTECT SOIL, HELP INDUSTRY -- T’usa Sinmun, 2 Dec 49 


The cabinet of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea has approved 
regulations governing the resettlement in industry of hwachon nongain [settlers 
who clear "fire-fields," presumably areas burned off for temporary cultivation; 
hereinafter referred to as settlers/. These rules apply to settlers who culti- 
vate land 15 degrees or more in inclination and by such cultivation cause flood, 
landslide, erosion and other natural damages to the land. 


Encouraging these settlers to seek jobs in factories would protect fields 
and mountains and at the same time, would gain more workers for factories. Fur- 


thermore, it is expected that the economic and cultural standards of these far- 
mers would be raised considerably by transferring them to industry.
