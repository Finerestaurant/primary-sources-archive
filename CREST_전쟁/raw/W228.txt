1. The Rumanian authorities have ordered the organization of "spontaneous" 
demonstrations in favor of Northern Korea in all parts of the country. 


2. Appeals are boing made to young men to volunteer to fight "for liberation 
of Korea from the yoke of the American invader." Recruiting cormissions 
have been created to enroll such volunteers. High pay has boen promised 
to their families. It is believed that these payments will be made from 
the World Peace Fund (Fondul Mondial al Poi). 


3, Cormunist appeals for volunteers and promises of financial reward have had 
very little effect on the population up to the present time.
