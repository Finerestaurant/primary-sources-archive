A. Establishment of Diplomatic and Cultural Relations 


Since its liberation from Japan, over 200,000 volumes of Soviet publica- 
tions have been distributed in the northern half of the People's Republic. 
These include the literary works of Generalissimo Stalin, notably . A Short 


History of the Soviet Communist Party (Bolshevik), and selections from Lenin 
and Marx. 


The establishment of diplomatic relations between the USSR and the Demo- 
cratic People's Republic of Korea on 12 October 1948, and the conclusion of the 
Beonomie and Cultural Exchange Pact in March 1949, have accelerated the pace of 
cultural intercourse and mutual assistances between the two nations.

Every committee member, who also belongs to the society, 4s an authority in his 
field, There are 504 such scholars and cultural leaders working for the research 
committees.

D. Korean-Soviet Friendship and Soviet Cultural Week 

To strengthen friendship between Korea and the USSR and to accelerate fur- 
ther assimilation of Soviet culture, tre Korean-Soviet Friendship and Soviet Cul- 
tural Week yas observed during 14 - 23 October 1949. Workers and farmers alike 
celebrated the week by increasing production or by establishing new and improv- 
ing the exieting cultural facilities. In celebration of the Korean-Soviet 
Friendship and Soviet Cultural Week conventions were held on 43 October 19!) in 
the northern half of the People's Republic at 10,935 places. Some 2,823 ,089 
persons participated dn these conventions. Each c vention sent a message of 
gratitude to the great Generalissimo Stalin. 


Over 120,540 lectures and round-table discussions were held during the 
week and were attended by 8,236,780 persons. Through these meetings , tthe devel:- 
opment of industries, the kolkhoz farm system in the USSR, and the happy Life 
of the Soviet people under the Soviet Socialist system were introduced to the 
Korean people. Various types of “xhibitions concerning the USSR were shown at 
over 24,898 pleces to some 8,019,664 persons. During the week, the literary and 
art groups Rave 83 public lectures on the works of Soviet writers and artists. 
A total of 7,043 organizations sponsored numerous public performances of plays, 
music, and dancing before 3,820,000 persons.
