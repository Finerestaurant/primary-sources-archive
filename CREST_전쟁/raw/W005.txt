NORTH KOREANS CONTRIBUTE FOR PLANES, TANKS; 
CLAIM POPULAR SUPPORT FOR SOUTH KOREA GUERRILLAS 


DONA'TE 200 MILLION WON FOR WAR MATERIEL -- Chokuk Chonson, 26 Nov hg 


According to an announcement by the Fatherland Defense Aid Society, the 
total value of contributions in cash and kind made by the people of North 
Korea for purchasing airplanes and tanks reached 200 million won as of 20 Novem- 
ber, 1949. This amount included 40 million won in cash, 25,000 bags [51,190 US 
pushels/ of grains, and 3,000 articles of various commodities. 


Also, as evidence of the. people's untiring enthusiasm for Fatherland de- 
fense, the merchants in Hamhung city who felt the need of strengthening naval 
defense, decided to donate 10,500,000 won to the People's Army [sic/ for con- 
struction of naval vessels. 


CLIDER TRAINEES GRADUATE -- T'usa Sinmun, 5 Jan 50 


Graduation exercises for the first group of elementary glider trainees took 
place for 29 December 1949 at the P'yongyang race track. The students received 
certificates of accomplishment from Yi Yong-kun, chairman of the P'yongan Namdo 
chapter of the Fatherland Defense Aid Society. After presenting the certificates, — 
Yi praised the trainees for their brilliant accomplishment in glider training in 
an amazingly short period, which, he said, was made possible only by their purn- 


ing enthusiasm and patriotism.
