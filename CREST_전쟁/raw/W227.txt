APPENDIX 


Rules Governing the Language Examination Committee 


l. The function of the Language Examination Committee shall be to § 
standardize the usage of technical terminology and to limit the use of 
Chinese characters to the practical minimum. , 


2. Subcommittees, specialized in different technical fields, shall 
be assigned to the respective bureau related to each field within the Min- 
istry of Education, as follows: : 


a. Subcommittees on Chinese characters and literary terms, to 
the Bureau of Compilation Control. 


b. Subcommittees on the terminology for mathematics, physics 
and astronomy, chemistry, geography and geology, history and archeology, 
and pedagogy, to the Bureau of Common Education. 


c. Subcommittees on the terminology for art, economics, and 
biology, to the Bureau of Technical Education. 


d. Subcommittees on the terminology for engineering, agriculture, 
forestry, fishery, medicine, law, philosophy, transportation and communica- 
tions, to the Bureau of Higher Education. iN 


Each subcommittee shall be composed of the members who are authorities 
in the field, and shall have a chairman, two vice chairmen, two secretaries, 
and several committee members, all of whom must be appointed by the Minister 
of Education. A majority vote shall be required for any decision made by the 
Language Examination Committee or subcommittees to be valid.
