The braveness Of our men, which gives our army unequaled strength, has been 
well demonstrated. Ina July 1949 battle, a pullet pierced the neck of Comrade 
Yi Chong-pin. In spite of the loss of much blood, he kept on fighting. He 
finally died most heroically by plugging the aperture of an enemy pillbox which 
wag hindering our advance with his own body. 


Comrade Kim Ch'ang-sop paved his comrades by jumping upon an enemy soldier 
who was just about to toss & hand grenade into our pillbox. Both he and the 
enemy were killed. : 


Wherein lies the real source of power of our army? The solid and steadfast 
political and thought conceptions of our soldiers are the basic foundation of 
our military strength. Our power lies in the democratic social system of the 
People's Republic. In our republic, the interest of the state is the interest 
of the individuals; the two are never separated. 


In contrast, the officers and troops in the so-called "National Defense 
Arny" of South Korea fight for ambiguous objectives, and consequently have no 
confidence in themselves. We find no instances of braveness comparable. to 
ours in their army. Such a Jack of confidence drives many puppet troops to 
revolt or desert from their line to join our guerrilla forces. For this reason 
along we can say with assurance that our military power 4s decidedly superior 


to theirs. 


In spite of this, the country-selli _ gang of South Korea has provocatively 
stated "War is the only answer to the problem of the North-South split. ‘Lhe 
year 1949 is for our preparation and the year 1950 for recovery of the lost ter- 
ritories.! Thus, the South Korean gang actually started preparations for its 
aggression; they effected military conscription, organized Youth Defense Corps, 
and engaged in other acts against us. . 


Today, our only choice is to challenge such an aggresssion, We feel keenly 
~ that the job of strengthening our military power has s tremendous significance, 
for it means safeguarding our freedom and democracy and liberating the suppressed 
people of South Korea. Let us perfect our preparations for this sacred mission. 
“fhe job of strengthening our military power is the job of all the people. We 
ask for your active support. 


The weapons, bullets, provisions, clothing, and other necessary military 
supplies are adequately provided for each member of the People's Arny, Constanbu- 
lary, and Security Force. Your joo is to help the soldiers' families, and co- 
operate with the Fatherland Defense Aid Society under the civil mobilization 
order so that our men can execute their missions without reserve. 


In this moment of crisis, let us band ourselves together around our leader 
Kim Il-song, and strive for the unification and sovereign independence of our’ 
Fatherland in defense of our freedom and democracy.
