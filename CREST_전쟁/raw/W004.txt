USSR TRANSFERS RED CROSS HOSPITALS -- Chokuk Chongon, 26 Nov 49 


An official ceremony marking the formal transfer of all of the 15 USSR 
Red Cross hospitals in North Korea to the government of the. People's Republic 
of Korea took place on 12 November 19h9 at the Ministry of Public Health in 
Pyongyang. The transfer entailed no obligation whatsoever on the part of 
the North Korean government as the hospitals were transferred gratuitously by 
the USSR Red Cross. : : : 


Tae USSR Red Cross hospitals were opened in 1946 in tue major cities and 
industrial centers in North Korea, and by June 1949, over 700,000 Korean pa- 
tients were treated or hospitalized by these hospitals, at a fee less than 
one fifth of cost. Their contribution to the development of the public health 
program of the People's Republic has been tremendous.
