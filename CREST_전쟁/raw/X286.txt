SAY SOVIETS FATL TO SUPPLY SUFFICIENT WAR MATERIEL -- Hong Kong, Kung-shang 
Jihepao, 11 Jul 51 


Taihoku, 10 July -= It is reported that one of the principal reasons why 
the Sino-Korean forces have agreed to terminate the ‘Korean conflict at the earli- 
est possible date is because the USSR is finding it impossible to furnish 5 mile 
lion Communist soldiers with new equipment within the specified time. It is 
further reported that the Chinese Communist forces have been greatly weakened 
by the lack of military supplies from the USSR and that they must be reorganized 
and rearmed to oppose any possible attack the KMI forces may launch. 


300,000 CHINESE CASUALTIES NOW RECUPERATING -- Hong Kong, Kung-shang Jih-pao, 
2 Jul 51 


Taihoku, 1 July -° The KMT Defense Ministry estimates that some 300,000 
Chinese Communist soldiers are recovering from injuries received in the Korean 
conflict, Of this total, approximately 50,000 are in Kwangtung hospitals.
