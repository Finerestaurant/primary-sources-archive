GETS MILITARY SUPPLIES IN EXCHANGE FOR WOMEN -- P'yonghwa Sinmun, 17 May 50 


It has been confirmed that the North Korean government has been sending 
large numbers of young Korean women to the USSR as compensation for the mili- 
tary supplies from the USSR. All women of certain age groups, whether married 
or single, with the exception of those who are married to Soviet citizens, are 
being conscripted. 


The People's Republic recently inaugurated a new tax called "Adult Educa- 
tion Rice." The purpose is to feed the expanding People's Army. This tax is © 
in addition to other taxes-in-kind, known as "Patriotic Rice" and "National Re- 
construction Rice," which have been levied upon farmers to alleviate the food 
crisis in North Korea during the spring of 1950.

P'yongyang, 26 June (Hsin-hua) -- The State Planning Board of the People's 
Republic of Korea is scheduled to submit to the cabinet, on 15 July 1950, the 
draft for a new People's Economic Program covering the period 1951 - 1953. The 
cabinet members concerned and all the provincial people's committees were re- 
quested to make necessary preparations for the new Three-Year People's Economic
