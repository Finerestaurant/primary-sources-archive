FUNCTIONS AND FEES OF NORTH KOREAN VETERINARY HOSPITALS 


Functions of Veterinary Hospitals 


According to Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry Ordinance No el, veteri- 
nary hospitals, under the supervision of the city and county people's committees, 
shall perform the following functions: (1) examine and treat livestock, (2) 
prevent and control livestock epidemics, (3) perform autops'iason livestock, and 
(4) promote livestock health. : 


There shall be one veterinary hospital in each area with a livestock popula- 
tion of 2,000 head. The hospitals shall be classified into Class A, which are 
supervised by a city people's committee; Class B, by the county people's commit- 
tee; and Class C, by a lower administrative unit. However, the. claseification 
of any hospital is subject to change at the discretion of the provincial people's 


committee concerned.
