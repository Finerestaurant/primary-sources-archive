LICENSES FOR PRIVATE FOREIGN TRADE IN NORTE KOREA 


According to Cabinet Decision No 68, the Rules Governing the Issuance of 
Licenses for Private Foreign Trade are hereby approved and adopted by the cabi- 
net of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea. 


Kim Il-song, Premier 
Chang Si-u, Minister of Commerce 


Rules Governing the Issuance of Licenses 
for Private Foreign Trade 


Private corporations and individuals may engage in foreign trade with per- 
mission of the Ministry of Commerce. The applicant may submit the standard ap- 
plication forms to the Bureau of Foreign Trade, Ministry of Commerce, to obtain 


the license. 


Export and import items shall be designated by, and shall remain under the 
control of, the Ministry of Commerce. A permit must be obtained in advance for 
each item for import and export. A report must be made to the bureau, within 
2 weeks after import or export, as to the quantity, value, specifications, 
nature of sale or purchase, etc., of the commodities.
