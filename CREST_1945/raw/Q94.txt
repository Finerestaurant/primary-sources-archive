SOVIET VESSELS PLY BETWEEN NORTH KOREA AND CHINA 


ASSERTS NORTH KOREA SHIPS IRON TO CHINA -- Tong-A Ilbo, 26 Feb 50 


The frequent passage of Soviet vessels, heavily laden with cargo, to and from 
North Korean and Commmist-occupied China via eastern coast of South Korea has 
been noticed lately. Kim Song~sam, Deputy Chief of Naval Operations of the Taehan 
Republic, said that the number of the Soviet vessels sailing along the eastern 
coast of South Korea has been increasing, and that all of the ships sailing from 
Hamgyong Provinces, North Korea, to Communist-occupied China are loaded with car- 
go, whereas the returning ships are usually empty. Kim asserted that the North 
Korean Reds are apparently shipping large quantities of iron materials to China. 


PEOPLE'S RESENTMENT BRINGS ECONOMIC DISORDER -- Kyong Hyang Sinmm, 26 Feb 50 


The economic programs put into effect by the North Korean puppet regime have 
failed due to relentless mistreatment of the workers, who demonstrate their re- 
sentment against the Commmists in the form of strikes and sabotage. 


It has been reported that recently some railroad workers went on strike, 
causing a number of locomotives to remain idle for 30 days. Another group of train- 
men deliberately refrained from completing their working quotas, as a complaint 
against profiteers who made undue profits. 


Also, it has been reported that unemployment is mounting, the food situ- 
ation is being aggravated, and the intellectuals are longing for a free economy 
more and more. Because of the drastic increase of business taxes since September 
1947, many merchants have closed their shops and quit business altogether. These 
merchants hope for an early collapse of the Commuaist puppet regime. 


Anti-~Commnist incidents have been reported frequently among the tax-burdened 
agrarian populace. 


Ignoring all these unhappy economic conditions, the North Korean Labor Party, 
which is the power behind the puppet regime, has been endeavoring to "militarize” 
the government, strengthen the People's Committee, and promote friendship with the
