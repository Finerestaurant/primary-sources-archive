RED CHINA CHARGES SOUTH KOREA 
WITH OPPRESSION OF OVERSEAS CHINESE 
South Korea, the most recent target of Chinese Communist propa- 
ganda, is being accused of oppressing overseas Chinese in that country. 
This charge was made by the Hsin-hua in a United Press dispatch from 
Hong Kong which appeared in the 7 March Nan-ch'iao Jih-pao, The Chi- 
nese Communist news agency said that under the pretext of checking 
smuggling activities, South Korean special agents are attaching or con- 
fiscating the goods of overseas Chinese. It further charged that land 
which overseas Chinese own or rent has been seized by the government 
and the business licenses of many have been canceled. 
The US was implicated when the Peiping Radio denounced the "US- 
controlled" South Korean government of Yi Sung-man (Syngman Rhee) for 
persecuting overseas Chinese. A United Press report from Tokyo of this 
broadcast was published in the 8 March Nan-ch'1iao Jih-pao.
