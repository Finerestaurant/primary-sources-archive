TEACHERS TO COOPERATE WITH EDUCATION PROGRAM -- T'usa Sinmun, 10 Jan 50 


On 7 January 1950, a convention was held for the teachers of the Chi- 
nese schools at the First People's School for Chinese, in P'yongyang. At 
the convention, Kim Yun-ho, Deputy Director of Education of the P'yongyang yd 
City People's Committee, reported on the accomplishments made in education fe 
for the Chinese residents in P'yongyang. The president of the P'yongyang : 
chapter of the Federation of Chinese Residents in North Korea and the direc- 
tor of the Bureau of Chinese Resident Education delivered congratulatory 
speeches, 


The Chinese teachers expressed their gratitude to the people of North 
Korea whose generous contributions made the school facilities available to 
the Chinese children. The teachers pledged to take good care of the schools. 
They also expressed appreciation to the government of the People's Republic 
of Korea for its guidances, and promised full cooperation in the forth- 
coming compulsory education program which wil], be equally applicable to the 
children of Chinese residents as well as to the Korean children. 


Kim Yun-ho encouraged the teachers by saying that they have an inmensely 
important role in the promotion of friendly relations between the two coun- 
tries, and that they must constantly strive to carry out that role satisfac- 
torily. He said that the friendship of the two countries will last for a 
long time if we properly guide the next generation of these countries toward 
@ common goal.

CHINESE SURGEON: ATTEND CONVENTION IN P'YONGYANG -- T'usa Sinmun, 8 Jan 50 


On 6 January 1950, the Ministry of Public Health of the People's Republic 
sponsored a New Year's party in honor of the group of Chinese surgeons led by 
Chu T'aek-so, chief surgeon of the Peiping Hospital, who have been visiting 
North Korea to attend the surgical science convention held in P'yongyang. 


Yi Tong-hwa, Vice-Minister of Public Health, Ch'oe Ch'ang-sok, Director 
of Foreign Affairs Bureau, public health officials, and many outstanding Kor- 


ean surgeons attended the party and extended enthusiastic welcomes to the 
Chinese.
