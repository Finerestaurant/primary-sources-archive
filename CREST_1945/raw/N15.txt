Delays in traveling are frequent and may last from a few hours to several days. I 
One cause of delay is the inferior grade of coal ané oil that the railroads are 
forced to use. Soviet trains have priority over other trains and it is often 
necessary to wait for Soviet freight and passenger trains to pass. On 12 February 
1947 a delay of over five hours occurred at Yangdok (Yotoku) (126-54, 39-10) when 
the engine of a Korean passenger train was- removed and attached to a Soviet freight 
train. The Korean stationmaster at Yangdok explained to the passengers that 
Soviets have priority over Korean passengers and that it was necessary to wait for 
another engine to arrive.
