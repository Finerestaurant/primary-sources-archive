deaf, and aduit education schools, was started on 5 January throughout North 
Korea. This special inductrination program for teachers is significant in 
view of the forthcoming universal compulsory educecion to be effected in the

TO EXPAND ADULT EDUCATION -- T'usa Sinmun, 21 Jan 50 


The P'yongyang City Adult Education Guidance Committee announced, at a 
Meeting on 16 January in the conference room of the P'yongyang City People's 
Committee, that the committee completed the adult education program for 18,902 
persons during 1949. The committee has decided to expand the program exten- 
sively this year by carrying the accelerated adult education program into fac- 
tories and the concentrated residential districts. The committee also decided 
to lower the minimum enroliment requirement to ten students to a class to open 
more classes. 


CABINET DECISION PROMOTES ADULT EDUCATION -- T'usa Sinmun, 4 Jan 50 


The Third Winter Adult Re-Education Program has been carried out in ac- 
cordance with Cabinet Order No 227, as “one of the most significant democratic 
projects for all people." The program has been attaining good results. 


The state examinations for adult schools and Hankul schools (schools for 
illiterates) for those who will complete the requirements for graduation this 
winter and for the candidates for advanced classes were held on 26 and 27 Decem- 
per 1949 throughout North Korea.
