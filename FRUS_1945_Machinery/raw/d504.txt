Moscow, April 18, 1945—8 p.m. 
 [Received April 18—1:05 p.m.]

1244. We have received no reply to the Ambassador’s letter to Molotov dated April 15, which was sent in compliance with Department’s 864 April 14, noon. [주10]

Under the circumstances I am informing Foreign Office that we are proceeding with the acceptance of the German proposal insofar as it relates to American prisoners of war.

[Kennan]
