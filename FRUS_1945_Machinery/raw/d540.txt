London, April 11, 1945.

940. Your No. 734 [주99] about Crosswords. [주1] I send you a private message I have received from Stalin covering the official telegram which he has sent to you with copy to me. [주2] I have a feeling that this is about the best we are going to get out of them, and certainly it is as near as they can get to an apology.

However, before considering any answer at all from His Majesty’s Government, please tell me how you think the matter should be handled so that we may keep in line together.
