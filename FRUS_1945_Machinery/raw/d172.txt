Washington, April 5, 1945—7 p.m.

2662. Substance of Department’s 2577, April 3, 8 p.m. [주29] and a copy of your 3405, April 4, noon were communicated to the President. In reply he has sent the following directive: “I do not wish any document or proposal changing the unconditional surrender terms”.
