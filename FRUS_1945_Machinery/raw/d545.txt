[Washington,] April 12, 1945.

230. Personal for Ambassador Harriman. Replying to your message 1210311 have replied to Churchill’s 940 by quoting my message to Stalin. [주12] Churchill is therefore fully informed and there is no necessity of your delaying delivery of my message to Stalin.

Your second question. I do not wish to delete the word “minor” as it is my desire to consider the Berne misunderstanding a minor incident.

Roosevelt
