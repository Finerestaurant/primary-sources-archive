London, February 2, 1945—7 p.m. 
 [Received February 2—6:12 p.m.]

173. Comea 170. In accordance with instructions received from the Secretary I have today addressed identic letters to the Soviet and UK representatives on the European Advisory Commission informing them of US approval of the protocol on zones of occupation in Germany, of September 12, 194, modified and completed by the agreement regarding amendments to the above-mentioned protocol, of November 14, 194.

Winant
