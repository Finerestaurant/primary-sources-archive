Washington, January 23, 1945—1 p.m.

502. Referring to the Department’s telegram No. 153, January 6, midnight, [주35] the President has now approved the agreement for control machinery for Germany. Accordingly, you are authorized to give formal notification of this Government’s approval of the agreement to your colleagues on EAC. [주36]

Grew
