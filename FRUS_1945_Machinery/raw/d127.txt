London, January 2, 1945—11 p.m. 
 [Received January 2—9:17 p.m.]

57. Personal for the Secretary and Assistant Secretary Dunn. I find that I have failed to give a final reply to your 10506 (December 16, midnight) [주93] after sending you my 11326 (December 20, 8 p.m.). [주94] The day Mr. Eden [주95] returned from Athens I asked him to take up this matter with the British Chiefs of Staff as it was blocking our acceptance of the protocol on zones in Germany and was probably responsible for the delay in acceptance by our Government of the agreement on control machinery. [주96] I reminded him that on his return from the last meeting at Quebec [주97] he had told me of the President’s generosity in agreeing to the British occupation of the northwestern zone. I told him I thought it was a poor return, in view of this concession, not to grant us control of Bremen and Bremerhaven and the necessary rail, highway and canal facilities to supply our [161면] forces in the southern zone. Eden told me he would take the matter up at once and try to get immediate action. I did this following receipt of a cablegram (War 84027, Hilldring [주98] to Peabody for Meyer, [주99] December 29, subject: Control Zones) stating that the British Chiefs’ counter-proposal was unsatisfactory to the United States Joint Chiefs of Staff.

Winant
